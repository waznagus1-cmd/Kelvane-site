Before you come in

Still not sure? Write to the shop and an artist will answer, usually the same day.

Do you take walk-ins?

Thursday to Saturday, from flash only, when an artist has a gap. Booking online is the only way to be sure of a chair.

How old do I need to be?

18 or older, with a valid photo ID on the day. No exceptions, including with a parent present.

Can I change a flash piece?

Small changes, like color or a name in a banner, are fine. Bigger changes become a custom piece and are priced by the hour.

When do I see my custom sketch?

Your artist sends it two to five days before your session. You can ask for one round of changes at no extra cost.

How do I take care of it after?

You leave with written aftercare and a second-skin bandage. Touch-ups in the first three months are free.
