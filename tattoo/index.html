How deposits work

It holds your date

Your deposit reserves the artist's time and pays for the design work done before your session.

It comes off the price

The full deposit is taken off your final total on the day. It's never an extra charge.

Plans change

Reschedule free up to 48 hours before. Later changes or no-shows keep the deposit.

Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

On Saturdays from 12 to 6 pm, for small flash pieces only. Everything else is by appointment.

How old do I need to be?

18 or older, with a valid photo ID on the day of your session. No exceptions.

When will I see my design?

Your artist sends a first sketch or questions within 48 hours of booking, and you'll see the final design at your session.

Can I bring a friend?

One guest is welcome for sessions up to three hours. For full-day sessions, please come alone.
