Before your first visit

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. A 20-minute consultation is free and needs no deposit. If you decide to be treated the same day, we quote the price before we start.

What happens to my deposit?

It comes off your bill on the day. Cancel or reschedule more than 24 hours before and it's refunded to your card in full.

Do you accept insurance?

Aesthetic treatments aren't covered by insurance. We accept all major cards, HSA and FSA cards where eligible, and monthly payments through Cherry.

Can I be treated if I'm pregnant or breastfeeding?

We don't perform injectables or laser treatments during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Some facials are safe, and your clinician will tell you which.

Should I tip?

No. Our clinicians are medical professionals and tipping isn't expected.
