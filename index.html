Questions

Do I need to change my booking software?

No. We work with what you already use, including Boulevard, Vagaro, Mangomint, Mindbody, Square and Fresha. If your current setup can't take deposits, we'll show you the simplest way to turn them on.

What do you need from me?

A 45-minute interview, your logo, your current prices and photos of your space and team. If you don't have good photos, we'll suggest a local photographer.

Who owns the website?

Your domain is always yours. If you pay once, the site is yours from day one. On the monthly plan, it becomes fully yours after 24 months, or earlier if you pay the remaining balance.

What's the difference between paying once and monthly?

The website is the same. Paying once costs less over time. Paying monthly means $0 down, with hosting and updates included, so you can launch now and pay as the site brings in bookings.

What about client data, consent forms and HIPAA?

Client information, intake and consent forms stay inside your booking software, which handles compliance. Your website never stores medical records or ID documents.

Can you work with my existing website?

Usually we rebuild it, because it's faster and cheaper than patching an old design. We keep your domain and anything that already ranks well on Google.

How do we pay?

By bank transfer or card, in US dollars. Pay once in two parts, half to start and half at launch, or monthly with $0 down.
